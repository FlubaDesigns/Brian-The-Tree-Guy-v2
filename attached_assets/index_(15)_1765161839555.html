Kingdom Connects operates on a faith-based tithing model. 10% of all Pro subscription revenue goes directly back to churches and ministries. When you upgrade to Pro or refer a business owner, you're not just growing your business—you're funding outreach, supporting local congregations, and advancing God's Kingdom.

What is Kingdom Connects?

A community-powered directory where church members and faith-aligned business owners connect, grow, and support each other. Discover trusted providers and help small businesses thrive while building God's Kingdom.

Is it free?

Yes! Listing your business is always free for church members. We offer optional Pro features ($9-12/month) for enhanced visibility, but the core directory will always be free. Plus, 10% of all revenue goes back to churches and outreach programs.

Why people use Kingdom Connects

  • Local, faith-aligned businesses curated by the community
  • Support fellow believers while getting quality service
  • Free business listings that are never deleted
  • 10% of revenue goes back to churches and ministries
  • Clean, mobile-friendly browsing and quick contact
